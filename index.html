Who We Are

We're a procurement and business development firm based in Charlotte, North Carolina.

We handle the contract side for clients who need services but don't want to deal with the vendor management. Our team finds qualified companies, manages the paperwork, and ensures everyone gets paid.

By serving as the essential link between clients and service providers, we streamline operations and eliminate the procurement headaches that slow down business.

What We Do

Vendor Management

We maintain relationships with qualified service companies across multiple sectors, ensuring you have access to reliable vendors when you need them.

Contract Management

From initial sourcing to final execution, we handle all contract documentation, compliance requirements, and administrative details.

Payment Processing

We manage the entire payment cycle, ensuring vendors are compensated promptly and clients maintain clear financial records.
